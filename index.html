Art. I

About

Profile

I'm a final-year B.B.A. LL.B. (Hons.) student at GD Goenka University, specialising in Arbitration and Cyber Law, with a growing focus on international commercial law, arbitration, and cross-border dispute resolution. That interest has developed through legal research, mediation practice, and internships that have exposed me to both Indian and international legal frameworks.

I currently work as a Trainee Mediator with George & George ADR Group, a US-based ADR organisation, supporting the resolution of consumer disputes under an American mediation framework, and as Course Coordinator with YCM India, coordinating internationally delivered mediation training involving participants and mediators from multiple jurisdictions. Alongside this, I hold leadership positions across my university's Centre for Conflict Resolution, Centre for Corporate Law, Moot Court Committee, and Trial Advocacy Committee.

In April 2026, I presented "Disputes Without Borders: Rethinking Enforcement in Cross-Border Counterfeiting" at GD Goenka University, and the following month presented "Identity without Accountability: Third Party Funding Regulation" at Jamia Hamdard University - both reflecting my growing interest in how commercial disputes are resolved and enforced across borders.

Through internships, mediation work, legal research, and leadership roles, I have tried to build experience that combines legal analysis with practical problem solving. I enjoy work that requires careful research, structured thinking, and collaboration across different legal and professional environments, and I hope to continue developing these skills through opportunities in commercial law and international dispute resolution.
